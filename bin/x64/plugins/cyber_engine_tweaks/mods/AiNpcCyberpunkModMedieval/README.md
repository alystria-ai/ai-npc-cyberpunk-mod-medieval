# Medieval AI NPCs

A content pack for AI NPC Cyberpunk Mod. It turns Night City into a medieval city and every person in it into a medieval townsperson, knight, sellsword, monk, bard or lord. Judy is still Judy, Jackie is still Jackie, and Rogue is still the queen of the Afterlife, but the Afterlife is now a sellswords' hall, cars are iron steeds, guns are fire-lances, netrunners are sorcerers and the NCPD is the Watch.

## What changes

- All 126 story characters and all 80 ordinary townsfolk kinds (gangers, vendors, guards, bartenders and so on) get a new medieval bio, speaking style and sample lines. The facts, family, friends, loyalties and looks of each character are kept, only recast.
- Every character gets a medieval name, such as Judith Alvares, Jackie of Heywood or Rogue, Queen of the Afterlife. The old name still works when you talk, type or search. The game shows the new names after the next game start or save load.
- Random passers-by get period names.
- Characters understand modern talk and answer in medieval terms. Ask for a lift to Kabuki and you get an offer of an iron steed.
- Voices stay the game's actors. Only the words change.
- The pack keeps its own memory, so talks with the medieval characters do not mix with your normal ones.

## What it works with

- Mistral, Local AI and Custom: everything above.
- Convai: every character has its own medieval Convai character, with the new bio, name and memory. Replies in a language other than English use the normal multilingual characters plus the pack's world rules, so they are lighter there.

## Needs

AI NPC Cyberpunk Mod 0.3.2 or newer (the add-on API 1.1).

## Install

1. Download the zip and extract it into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder, so that the folder lands in bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/ as AiNpcCyberpunkModMedieval.
2. Start the game. Open Settings, then Content packs, and make sure Medieval AI NPCs is On.

## Turn it off

Settings, then Content packs, then switch Medieval AI NPCs to Off. It takes effect from the next reply. Everything goes back as it was. You can also delete the folder.

## Tips

- Your own edits in Settings, Edit characters, still win over the pack for that character.
- The pack works with the Mature content setting. It brings period oaths and bawdy words to the Mature wording.
- Small local models follow the style best on short replies. Long stories can slip into plain modern English now and then.
